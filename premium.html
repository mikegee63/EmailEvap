The small print. Premium is a monthly subscription billed by Stripe at $1.99 (USD) until you cancel. Cancel at any time from the billing page; your addresses keep receiving until the end of the period you paid for. Thirty days after a subscription ends, its addresses and stored messages are deleted permanently. Addresses are receive-only. Each address keeps its most recent 500 messages; older ones are dropped automatically. Anyone who knows one of your addresses can send mail to it, so do not use Premium for anything you would not want a stranger to be able to reach.
