What is EmailVanish?

EmailVanish gives you a free, working email address in one click, with no sign-up. Use it when a website insists on an email address but you do not want to hand over your real one: downloading a file, reading an article, joining public Wi-Fi or testing an app. Your real inbox stays out of their marketing lists and out of their next data breach.

How it works

  1. Copy the address above. It is random, so nobody else is using it.
  2. Paste it into the sign-up form. Messages appear on this page within about fifteen seconds. Images and tracking pixels are stripped out before you see anything.
  3. Walk away. Messages are deleted automatically ten minutes after the last one arrives. We keep no archive.

EmailVanish is receive-only and has no password, so anyone who knows an address can read it while it exists. It is ideal for throwaway sign-ups and the wrong choice for banking, work or accounts you want to keep. Read when to use a temporary address for a quick checklist, or how it works behind the scenes.

Frequently asked questions

Is EmailVanish free?

Yes. Temporary addresses are free and need no account. An optional Premium subscription keeps chosen addresses receiving mail for as long as you subscribe.

How long does my address last?

The page keeps showing your inbox for ten minutes. Messages are deleted ten minutes after the last one arrives, and Reset Timer gives you a brand-new address with a fresh ten minutes.

Can I send email or reply from this address?

No. EmailVanish is receive-only, which is also why it cannot be used to send spam.

Why did my confirmation email not arrive?

Some websites block temporary addresses, and a few send slowly. Keep this page open for a couple of minutes. If nothing arrives, the site probably blocks disposable email; see why sites do that and what to use instead.

Is it private?

Your real address stays hidden and we keep nothing after messages expire. But there is no password on a free inbox, so do not use one for anything confidential. Our privacy policy lists exactly what is logged.

Can I use it for any website?

For privacy, yes. For fraud, trial abuse, ban evasion or harassment, no: those uses break our acceptable use policy.
